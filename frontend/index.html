A MINUTE TO LEARN

Find your alignment.

You and ORBIT AI each control three pieces. Be the first to make a straight line through the center.

  1. Move one piece each turn. Follow a ring connection or a spoke to an empty neighboring point. No jumping or capturing.
  2. Only four lines win. Any two opposite outer points plus the center. Three pieces around the edge do not count.
  3. Make your minute count. You have 60 seconds across all your turns. Your clock pauses during AI turns; zero means an AI win.
  4. A fresh puzzle every time. The opening board has no existing or one-move wins for either side. A coin flip chooses who starts.

No legal moves means a draw. Repeated positions are allowed. Search settings can be changed between matches; larger depths are capped by a computation budget to keep the game responsive.
